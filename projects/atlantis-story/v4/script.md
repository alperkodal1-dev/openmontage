## Sources
- Plato, Timaeus 20d-25d; Critias 113c-121c (c. 360 BC): ring city, orichalcum, bronze walls, temple of Poseidon,
  "in a single day and night of misfortune", 9,000 years before Solon.
- Athanasius Kircher, Mundus Subterraneus (1665/1678), map of Atlantis.
- Ignatius Donnelly, Atlantis: The Antediluvian World (1882); US congressman from Minnesota.
- Herodotus, Thucydides and earlier writers do not mention Atlantis.
