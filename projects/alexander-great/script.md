## Sources (checked)
- Plutarch, On Tranquility of Mind 466D: Alexander weeps at Anaxarchus' talk of infinite worlds, "not yet lord of one".
- Plutarch, Life of Alexander 2-3 (Olympias, Zeus), 6 (Bucephalus); Arrian, Anabasis 2.3 (Gordian knot: sword, or the pin per Aristobulus).
- Battles: Granicus 334, Issus 333, Gaugamela 331, Hydaspes 326 BC; died in Babylon 323 BC, aged 32.
- "Alexander the Great" (Alexandrum Magnum) first attested in Plautus, Mostellaria (c. 200 BC).
