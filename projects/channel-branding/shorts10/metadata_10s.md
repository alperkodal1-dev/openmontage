## 2. Noah's Ark (noah_10s.mp4)
Title: The Bible Never Says Noah's Ark Landed on Ararat #shorts #bible #noahsark
Title (TR): İncil, Nuh'un Gemisi'nin Ağrı Dağı'na İndiğini Hiç Söylemez #shorts #nuhungemisi

The Bible never says Noah's Ark landed on Mount Ararat. Not once. Genesis 8:4 says the ark rested on "the mountains of Ararat". Mountains. Plural. And Ararat wasn't a peak: it was a kingdom, Urartu, around Lake Van.

So where did the ark really stop? Ararat or Cudi? 👇
