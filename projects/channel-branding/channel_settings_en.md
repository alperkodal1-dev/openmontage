## Channel description

Or So The Story Goes: the stories everyone thinks they know, traced back to the sources.

Short, cinematic history mysteries and myth-busters, built from real archive paintings, manuscripts, old maps and primary sources. Did Columbus prove the Earth was round? Did the Piri Reis map show Antarctica? Where did Noah's Ark really land? We follow the evidence, chapter by chapter, and let you decide: real or myth?

What you'll find here:
• History myths, checked against the sources shown on screen and listed in every description
• Ancient mysteries and lost civilizations, examined honestly
• Forgotten battles and rival empires, told like a film

New Shorts every week, and longer documentaries along the way. Every legend ends the same way here: "Or so the story goes." Then we open the records.

## Settings checklist

- Settings > Channel > Basic info: country stays the country you live in (it must match your AdSense/payment details; viewer country, not channel country, sets ad rates).
- Settings > Upload defaults > Advanced settings: Video language = English, Title and description language = English, Category = Education.
- Settings > Channel > Advanced settings: audience = "No, not made for kids".
- Customization > Basic info > Name: Or So The Story Goes. Handle: try @OrSoTheStoryGoes, then @SoTheStoryGoes or @OrSoTheStory.
- Customization > Branding: profile picture = avatar_goat_uh_v3_ring.jpg (the live one is still the first goat without "UH"); add a banner (2560x1440, safe area 1546x423) and a video watermark.
- Customization > Layout: add playlists (History Myths, Ancient Mysteries, Battles and Empires) and set a featured video.
- Per video, when uploading: "Altered or synthetic content" = Yes for any video with realistic AI-generated scenes (the Battle of Ankara video).
