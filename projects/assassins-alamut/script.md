## Sources (to verify before voicing)
- Marco Polo, *Il Milione* ch. on the Old Man of the Mountain (c. 1298); his route through Persia c. 1272.
- Alamut taken by Hasan-i Sabbah 1090; surrendered to Hülegü 1256; library burned by Juvayni (*Tarikh-i Jahangushay*).
- Nizam al-Mulk killed 14 Oct 1092 near Nahavand by a man disguised as a Sufi petitioner (Ibn al-Athir; Rashid al-Din).
- "Hashishi/hashishiyya" as a pejorative in Fatimid and Syrian sources; no Nizari source mentions hashish:
  F. Daftary, *The Assassin Legends* (1994); B. Lewis, *The Assassins* (1967).
- Saladin and the dagger: later biographical tradition (Kamal al-Din) about Sinan, 1176 — told as legend.
