## Spoken text (for the voiceover)

This Ottoman map from 1513 doesn't show Antarctica. The truth is stranger. In 1929, a German scholar in Topkapı Palace found a scrap of gazelle skin. Admiral Piri Reis drew it in Gallipoli, twenty-one years after Columbus sailed. Its margins are full of wonders. Monks who camped on a giant fish. One-horned oxen. Monsters seven spans long, "but they are harmless souls." In the 1960s, writers claimed its southern coast was Antarctica, mapped before the ice. A lost civilization. Some said aliens. But Antarctica has been frozen for millions of years, and no one saw it until 1820. Most historians read that coast as South America, bent to fit the skin. The real secret is in the notes. Piri Reis copied about twenty maps, one of them by Columbus. His uncle had captured a sailor who sailed with Columbus three times. Columbus's own map is lost. This may be its last echo. So which is stranger: a lost civilization, or a lost map? Real or myth? Tell me below.
