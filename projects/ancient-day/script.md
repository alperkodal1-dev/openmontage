## Sources
- R. B. Lee, "What hunters do for a living" (1968, Man the Hunter); Lee, The !Kung San (1979): ~2.4 days/week food getting; ~40-44 h with tool making and housework.
- M. Dyble et al., "Engagement in agricultural work is associated with reduced leisure time among Agta hunter-gatherers", Nature Human Behaviour 3, 792-796 (2019): ~10 h/week more leisure for foragers.
- P. Wiessner, "Embers of society: Firelight talk among the Ju/'hoansi Bushmen", PNAS 111(39) (2014): 81% of night conversations were stories vs 6% by day.
- N. Conard et al., "New flutes document the earliest musical tradition in southwestern Germany", Nature 460 (2009): Hohle Fels griffon-vulture radius flute, >35,000 years.
