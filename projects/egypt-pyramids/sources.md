# Sources (draft, to be checked before voicing)

- Great Pyramid of Khufu, c. 2560 BC, about 2.3 million blocks: Lehner, *The Complete Pyramids* (1997).
- Herodotus, *Histories* 2.124: 100,000 men in three-month shifts for twenty years (he does not call them slaves).
- Erich von Däniken, *Erinnerungen an die Zukunft* (1968; English *Chariots of the Gods?*).
- Abydos "helicopter": Temple of Seti I; a palimpsest, Ramesses II's titles re-carved over Seti I's.
- Dendera "light bulb": Hathor temple crypt reliefs (Ptolemaic); a lotus flower giving birth to a snake, a creation image.
- Saqqara bird: sycamore wood model, c. 200 BC, Egyptian Museum Cairo; read by most as a bird or toy.
- Orion correlation: Bauval & Gilbert, *The Orion Mystery* (1994); disputed by astronomers and Egyptologists.
- Workers' cemetery at Giza: found 1990 (a horse stumbled on a mud-brick wall), excavated by Zahi Hawass;
  burials with bread and beer offerings, healed fractures, near the king's pyramid.
- Work-gang names "Friends of Khufu", "Drunkards of Menkaure": Lehner & AERA (Ancient Egypt Research Associates).
- Wadi al-Jarf papyri: found 2013 by Pierre Tallet; Diary of Merer, Khufu's reign, Tura limestone shipped to Giza;
  the oldest inscribed papyri known.
- (Possible extra) Tomb of Djehutihotep, Deir el-Bersha, c. 1850 BC: colossus on a sledge, water poured on the sand;
  Fall et al., Physical Review Letters 2014 (wet sand halves the pulling force).

## v3 additions
- Granite beams over the King's Chamber, about 25-80 t: Lehner, *The Complete Pyramids*.
- Alignment to true north within about 3-4 arcminutes (~0.05°): Spence, Nature 408 (2000).
- "Power plant": Christopher Dunn, *The Giza Power Plant* (1998). Not accepted by Egyptologists.
- King's Chamber southern shaft aimed at Orion's belt c. 2500 BC: Trimble & Badawy (1964); the Pyramid Texts
  link the dead king with Orion/Osiris (Sah).
- Sarcophagus found empty (Arabic accounts of al-Ma'mun's entry, 9th c.); Khufu's mummy has never been found.
