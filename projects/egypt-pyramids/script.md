## Spoken text (for the voiceover)

This carving in an Egyptian temple seems to show a helicopter. In 1968, a Swiss writer said ancient astronauts had visited Egypt. His followers saw more clues. At Dendera, a snake in what looks like a giant bulb. At Saqqara, a wooden bird with glider wings. And the Great Pyramid: about two point three million blocks, aligned to true north. Some claim it was a power plant. Others, a stargate: one shaft once aimed at Orion's belt. In 1990, the story goes, a horse tripped on a buried wall. Beneath it lay the builders, in the shadow of their king's pyramid. Their gangs signed the stones: Friends of Khufu. Next door, Drunkards of Menkaure. A papyrus logbook tracks boats of limestone to Giza. Yet the king's sarcophagus was found empty. Later pyramid texts promise the king a staircase to the sky, to become Orion. A tomb... or a stairway to the stars? So what were the pyramids, really?
